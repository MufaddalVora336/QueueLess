System Architecture & Features

Designed for public service efficiency and reduced physical crowding.

⚡

Unified Queue

Online citizens and physical walk-ins are merged into a single sequential priority list. Fair, transparent order with no separate queues.

⏱️

Smart Arrival Window

Citizens receive an estimated 10-minute arrival window (e.g. 3:45 PM – 3:55 PM) so they do not wait in crowded halls.

📋

Service Readiness Checklist

Mandatory document verification checklist before booking. Citizens confirm their papers in advance, avoiding rejected turns.

🛡️

Queue Protection

Mobile verification and a limit of one active token per service prevents slot hoarding and fraudulent multiple bookings.
